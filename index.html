How Our System Works

2

Analyze with AI/ML

3

Predict Maintenance Risk

AI/ML OPPORTUNITY

Predictive Maintenance Analysis

The proposed system uses machine learning to analyze historical medical equipment data and identify patterns that may indicate future maintenance requirements.

Equipment parameters such as operating hours, temperature, usage frequency and previous maintenance history can be used as input features for prediction.

Based on the analysis, the system can classify equipment into different risk levels such as Low Risk, Medium Risk and High Risk.
